Privacy Policy

Yuyu – Daily Chinese Words · Effective date: 18 November 2026

Yuyu is made by Pongtop Oy ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Yuyu app. The short version: Yuyu keeps your learning on your phone and doesn't track you.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (language, level, widget style), your progress and the words you mark as learned are stored only on your device. We don't receive them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app. Yuyu has no account system and works offline.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or browsing activity. Yuyu contains no ads and no advertising or analytics trackers.

Subscriptions (Yuyu Plus)

If you start the free trial or subscribe, the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. We never see your payment card details.

To check whether your subscription is active, Yuyu uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase records (which product, when, and whether it's active). This information is used only to unlock Yuyu Plus, is not sold, and is not used for advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Yuyu is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because almost everything stays on your device, you control it: reset your progress in Settings or uninstall the app. To ask about or delete the subscription records held by RevenueCat, email us at support@lauskigames.com. If you're in the EU/EEA you have the rights given by the GDPR, including access, correction and deletion, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, mention it in the app's release notes.
