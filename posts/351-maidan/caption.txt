Today's word: 买单 (mǎidān) = to pay the bill 🐟🐟

Shout 买单 to get the bill 🧾 Fun fact: in China friends often fight over who gets to pay.

服务员，买单！
Fúwùyuán, mǎidān!
Waiter, the bill please!
