Today's word: 鸡蛋 (jīdàn) = egg 🐟🐟

Literally "chicken egg" 🥚 So which came first, the 鸡 or the 蛋?

我早饭吃两个鸡蛋。
Wǒ zǎofàn chī liǎng ge jīdàn.
I eat two eggs for breakfast.

Answer in the comments: 鸡 or 蛋? 🐔🥚
