Today's word: 草莓 (cǎoméi) = strawberry 🐟🐟

Literally "grass berry" 🍓 Because strawberries grow low, right by the grass.

我最喜欢草莓。
Wǒ zuì xǐhuan cǎoméi.
I like strawberries the most.
