Today's word: 弟弟 (dìdi) = younger brother 🐟🐟

弟弟 = little brother 👶 And 岁 is how you say someone's age: 十岁, ten years old.

我弟弟今年十岁。
Wǒ dìdi jīnnián shí suì.
My little brother is ten this year.

How old would you be in Chinese? 我…岁 👇
