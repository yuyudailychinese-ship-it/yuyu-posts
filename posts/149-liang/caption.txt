Today's word: 两 (liǎng) = two (of something) 🐟🐟

Two ways to say two! 二 for counting numbers, 两 for two of something: 两杯咖啡 ☕☕

我要两杯咖啡。
Wǒ yào liǎng bēi kāfēi.
I'd like two coffees.

Order like a pro: 我要两杯… 👇
