Today's word: 感动 (gǎndòng) = moved, touched 🐟🐟

感 (feel) + 动 (move) = moved, touched 🥹 Just like in English!

这个故事让我很感动。
Zhège gùshi ràng wǒ hěn gǎndòng.
This story really moved me.

Last thing that made you 感动? 🥹
