Today's word: 中国 (Zhōngguó) = China 🐟🐟

中国 = the "middle kingdom" 🇨🇳 And 国 is a border 囗 around jade 玉: a country guarding its treasure.

我想去中国旅游。
Wǒ xiǎng qù Zhōngguó lǚyóu.
I want to travel to China.
