Today's word: 零食 (língshí) = snack 🐟🐟

零 means zero or small bits, so snacks are "bits of food" 🍿 Zero calories? Sadly no.

我晚上不吃零食。
Wǒ wǎnshang bù chī língshí.
I don't eat snacks at night.
