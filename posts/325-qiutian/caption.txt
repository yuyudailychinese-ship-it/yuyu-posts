Today's word: 秋天 (qiūtiān) = autumn 🐟🐟

秋 = grain 禾 + fire 火 🍂 Harvest time, when fields turn the color of flames.

秋天的叶子很漂亮。
Qiūtiān de yèzi hěn piàoliang.
The leaves are beautiful in autumn.
