Today's word: 葡萄 (pútao) = grape 🐟🐟

Two characters, one sound: pú-tao 🍇 Add 酒 (alcohol) and you get wine.

这些葡萄有点儿酸。
Zhèxiē pútao yǒudiǎnr suān.
These grapes are a bit sour.

Red or white 葡萄酒? 🍷
