Today's word: 上网 (shàngwǎng) = to go online 🐟🐟

Literally "go on the net" 🕸️ And 网 even looks like a net!

我每天晚上上网。
Wǒ měitiān wǎnshang shàngwǎng.
I go online every evening.

How many hours do you 上网 a day? 🕸️
