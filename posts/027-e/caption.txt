Today's word: 饿 (è) = hungry 🐟🐟

Memory trick: 饿 = food 饣 + me 我 🍜 When I need food, I'm hungry.

我饿了，我们去吃饭吧！
Wǒ è le, wǒmen qù chīfàn ba!
I'm hungry, let's go eat!

What do you eat when you're 饿? 👇
