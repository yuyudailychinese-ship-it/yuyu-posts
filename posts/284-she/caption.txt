Today's word: 蛇 (shé) = snake 🐟🐟

蛇 has 虫 (bug) on the left 🐍 Old Chinese grouped snakes with creepy crawlies.

那里有一条蛇！
Nàlǐ yǒu yì tiáo shé!
There's a snake over there!
