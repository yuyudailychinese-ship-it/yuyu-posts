Today's word: 千 (qiān) = thousand 🐟🐟

千 = thousand 🔢 Fun: 千万 ("ten million") also means "absolutely, by all means".

我走了一千步。
Wǒ zǒule yìqiān bù.
I walked a thousand steps.
