Today's word: 放心 (fàngxīn) = to feel at ease, not worry 🐟🐟

担心 = carry your heart (worry). 放心 = put your heart down (relax) 😌 Beautiful pair.

放心吧，我会准时到。
Fàngxīn ba, wǒ huì zhǔnshí dào.
Don't worry, I'll be on time.
