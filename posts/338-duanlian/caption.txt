Today's word: 锻炼 (duànliàn) = to work out, to train 🐟🐟

Literally "forge and refine" your body 💪 Like a blacksmith with metal!

我每天锻炼身体。
Wǒ měitiān duànliàn shēntǐ.
I work out every day.

How do you 锻炼? 💪
