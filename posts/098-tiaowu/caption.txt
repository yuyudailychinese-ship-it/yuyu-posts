Today's word: 跳舞 (tiàowǔ) = to dance 🐟🐟

Literally "jump dance" 💃 Fun fact: in China, groups of aunties dance in parks every evening!

她很喜欢跳舞。
Tā hěn xǐhuan tiàowǔ.
She really loves dancing.
