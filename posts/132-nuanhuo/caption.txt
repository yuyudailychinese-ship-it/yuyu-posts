Today's word: 暖和 (nuǎnhuo) = warm 🐟🐟

暖和 = pleasantly warm 🌤️ Not 热 (hot), just cozy.

今天很暖和。
Jīntiān hěn nuǎnhuo.
It's nice and warm today.
