Today's word: 讨厌 (tǎoyàn) = to dislike, to hate; annoying 🐟🐟

讨厌 = to hate 😒 Said teasingly, 讨厌! means "you're so annoying!" (in a cute way).

我讨厌下雨天。
Wǒ tǎoyàn xiàyǔtiān.
I hate rainy days.

Something you 讨厌? 😒
