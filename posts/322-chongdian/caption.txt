Today's word: 充电 (chōngdiàn) = to charge (a battery) 🐟🐟

Literally "fill with electricity" 🔋 People also say it about themselves: studying = 充电!

我的手机要充电。
Wǒ de shǒujī yào chōngdiàn.
My phone needs charging.
