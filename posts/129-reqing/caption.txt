Today's word: 热情 (rèqíng) = warm, enthusiastic 🐟🐟

Literally "hot feelings" 🔥 A warm, welcoming person is 热情.

这里的人很热情。
Zhèlǐ de rén hěn rèqíng.
The people here are very warm.
