Today's word: 狗 (gǒu) = dog 🐟🐟

See the 犭 on the left? It shows up in lots of animal words: 猫, 狗, 猪 (pig). Spot the pattern 🐶

他家有一只小狗。
Tā jiā yǒu yì zhī xiǎo gǒu.
His family has a little dog.
