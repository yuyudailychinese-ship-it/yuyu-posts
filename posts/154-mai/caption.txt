Today's word: 买 (mǎi) = to buy 🐟🐟

买 (mǎi) = buy, 卖 (mài) = sell 🛍️ Same sound, different tone, one more stroke. Tricky pair!

我想买一个新手机。
Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yí ge xīn shǒujī.
I want to buy a new phone.

Last thing you 买-ed? 🛍️
