Today's word: 外国人 (wàiguórén) = foreigner 🐟🐟

Literally "outside-country person" 🌍 Perfect sentence for your first trip to China!

我是外国人，我在学中文。
Wǒ shì wàiguórén, wǒ zài xué Zhōngwén.
I'm a foreigner, I'm learning Chinese.
