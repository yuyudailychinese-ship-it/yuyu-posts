Today's word: 快 (kuài) = fast, quick 🐟🐟

快 = fast ⚡ 快点儿! = hurry up! Very useful with slow friends 😂

快点儿！我们要迟到了！
Kuài diǎnr! Wǒmen yào chídào le!
Hurry up! We're going to be late!
