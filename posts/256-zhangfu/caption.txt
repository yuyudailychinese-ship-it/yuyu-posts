Today's word: 丈夫 (zhàngfu) = husband 🐟🐟

丈夫 is the proper word for husband 💍 In everyday chat people say 老公.

她丈夫是老师。
Tā zhàngfu shì lǎoshī.
Her husband is a teacher.

老公 or 丈夫: which sounds better to you? 💍
