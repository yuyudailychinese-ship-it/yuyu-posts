Today's word: 星星 (xīngxing) = star 🐟🐟

星 = 日 (sun) + 生 (born) ✨ A star is born, literally!

天上有很多星星。
Tiānshang yǒu hěn duō xīngxing.
There are lots of stars in the sky.
