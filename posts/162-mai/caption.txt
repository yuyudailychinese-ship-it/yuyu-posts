Today's word: 卖 (mài) = to sell 🐟🐟

卖 (sell) has an extra 十 on top of 买 (buy) 💰 Memory trick: you sell what you have extra of.

这家店卖什么？
Zhè jiā diàn mài shénme?
What does this shop sell?
