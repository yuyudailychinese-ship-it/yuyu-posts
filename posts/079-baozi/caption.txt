Today's word: 包子 (bāozi) = steamed bun 🐟🐟

A fluffy steamed bun with filling inside 🥟 包 means to wrap, and that's exactly what it is.

这家的包子很好吃。
Zhè jiā de bāozi hěn hǎochī.
This place's steamed buns are delicious.
