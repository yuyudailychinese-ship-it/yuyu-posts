Today's word: 中秋节 (Zhōngqiūjié) = Mid-Autumn Festival 🐟🐟

Mid-Autumn Festival = full moon + mooncakes 🥮 Families gather to look at the moon.

中秋节我们吃月饼。
Zhōngqiūjié wǒmen chī yuèbing.
We eat mooncakes at Mid-Autumn Festival.
