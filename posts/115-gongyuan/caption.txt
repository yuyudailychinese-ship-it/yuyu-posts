Today's word: 公园 (gōngyuán) = park 🐟🐟

Literally "public garden" 🌳 Chinese parks are full of tai chi, dancing and chess in the mornings!

我们去公园散步吧。
Wǒmen qù gōngyuán sànbù ba.
Let's go for a walk in the park.
