Today's word: 远 (yuǎn) = far 🐟🐟

远 = far, 近 = near 📏 Both have the walking sign 辶 at the bottom left.

你家离这儿远吗？
Nǐ jiā lí zhèr yuǎn ma?
Is your home far from here?

Is China 远 from where you are? 📏
