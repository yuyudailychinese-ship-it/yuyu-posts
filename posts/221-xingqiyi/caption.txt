Today's word: 星期一 (xīngqīyī) = Monday 🐟🐟

Monday = "week one" 😩 Tuesday is 星期二, Wednesday 星期三… easy!

星期一我很忙。
Xīngqīyī wǒ hěn máng.
I'm very busy on Monday.

Comment 星期一 if you have the Monday blues 😩
