Today's word: 春节 (Chūnjié) = Spring Festival (Chinese New Year) 🐟🐟

Chinese New Year is called 春节, Spring Festival 🧧 Billions of trips home, the biggest migration on Earth!

春节我们回老家。
Chūnjié wǒmen huí lǎojiā.
For Spring Festival we go back to our hometown.
