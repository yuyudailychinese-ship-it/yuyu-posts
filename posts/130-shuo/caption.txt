Today's word: 说 (shuō) = to speak, to say 🐟🐟

说 has the speech sign 讠 on the left 🗣️ You'll see it in lots of talking words.

你会说中文吗？
Nǐ huì shuō Zhōngwén ma?
Can you speak Chinese?

Answer: 我会说一点儿中文! 🗣️
