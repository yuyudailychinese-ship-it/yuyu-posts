Today's word: 啤酒 (píjiǔ) = beer 🐟🐟

啤 just sounds like "beer", 酒 means alcohol 🍺 And when you toast, say 干杯: "dry the cup"!

我们去喝啤酒吧！
Wǒmen qù hē píjiǔ ba!
Let's go have a beer!
