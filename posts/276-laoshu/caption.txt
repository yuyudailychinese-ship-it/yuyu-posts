Today's word: 老鼠 (lǎoshǔ) = mouse, rat 🐟🐟

老鼠 = mouse or rat 🐭 It's the first animal of the Chinese zodiac. Legend says it rode the ox to win the race!

我怕老鼠。
Wǒ pà lǎoshǔ.
I'm afraid of mice.
