Today's word: 回家 (huíjiā) = to go home 🐟🐟

回家 = go home 🏡 回 looks like a little square inside a bigger one: back where you belong.

我要回家了。
Wǒ yào huí jiā le.
I'm heading home.

Best feeling: 回家 after a long day. Agree? 🏡
