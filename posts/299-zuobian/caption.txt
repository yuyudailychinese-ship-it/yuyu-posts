Today's word: 左边 (zuǒbian) = left side 🐟🐟

左 (left) and 右 (right) look similar 👈 Left has 工 at the bottom, right has 口.

银行在左边。
Yínháng zài zuǒbiān.
The bank is on the left.

Quick test: which is left, 左 or 右? 👈
