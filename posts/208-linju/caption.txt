Today's word: 邻居 (línjū) = neighbor 🐟🐟

邻居 = the people living next to you 🏘️ A good neighbor is gold.

我的邻居很安静。
Wǒ de línjū hěn ānjìng.
My neighbor is very quiet.

Are your 邻居 loud or quiet? 🏘️
