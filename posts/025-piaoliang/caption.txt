Today's word: 漂亮 (piàoliang) = beautiful, pretty 🐟🐟

亮 means bright ✨ So 漂亮 is pretty as in shining.

这件衣服很漂亮。
Zhè jiàn yīfu hěn piàoliang.
This outfit is really pretty.
