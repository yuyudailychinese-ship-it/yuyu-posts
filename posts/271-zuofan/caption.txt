Today's word: 做饭 (zuòfàn) = to cook 🐟🐟

Literally "make a meal" 👨‍🍳 And 很会做饭 means someone really knows their way around a kitchen.

我爸爸很会做饭。
Wǒ bàba hěn huì zuòfàn.
My dad is a great cook.

Who's the best cook in your family? 👨‍🍳
