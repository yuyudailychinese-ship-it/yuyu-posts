Today's word: 洗手间 (xǐshǒujiān) = restroom 🐟🐟

Literally "hand-washing room" 🚻 The most important travel sentence ever.

请问，洗手间在哪儿？
Qǐngwèn, xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr?
