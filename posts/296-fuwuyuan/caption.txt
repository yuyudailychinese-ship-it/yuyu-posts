Today's word: 服务员 (fúwùyuán) = waiter, attendant 🐟🐟

Literally "service staff" 🙋 In restaurants you can just call out 服务员! and someone comes.

服务员，请给我一杯水。
Fúwùyuán, qǐng gěi wǒ yì bēi shuǐ.
Waiter, a glass of water please.
