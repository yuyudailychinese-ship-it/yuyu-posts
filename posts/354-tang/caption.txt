Today's word: 躺 (tǎng) = to lie down 🐟🐟

躺 = lie down 🛌 Slang 躺平 ("lie flat") means opting out of the rat race.

我想躺在床上看书。
Wǒ xiǎng tǎng zài chuáng shang kàn shū.
I want to lie in bed and read.

Comment 躺 if you're in bed right now 🛌
