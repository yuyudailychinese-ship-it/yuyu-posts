Today's word: 牛奶 (niúnǎi) = milk 🐟🐟

Literally "cow milk" 🐄🥛 And the same 奶 is in 奶茶, milk tea!

我每天喝一杯牛奶。
Wǒ měitiān hē yì bēi niúnǎi.
I drink a glass of milk every day.

Milk in your tea: yes or no? 🥛
