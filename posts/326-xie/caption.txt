Today's word: 鞋 (xié) = shoe 🐟🐟

鞋 = shoe 👟 Shoes come in a pair, 一双鞋. Also: 革 on the left means leather!

这双鞋很舒服。
Zhè shuāng xié hěn shūfu.
These shoes are very comfortable.
