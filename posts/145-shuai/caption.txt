Today's word: 帅 (shuài) = handsome 🐟🐟

帅 = handsome 😎 And 帅哥 ("handsome brother") is how you call any good-looking guy.

你今天好帅！
Nǐ jīntiān hǎo shuài!
You look so handsome today!
