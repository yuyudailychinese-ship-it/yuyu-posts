Today's word: 肉 (ròu) = meat 🐟🐟

Add an animal in front and you've got a menu: 牛肉 (cow meat), 猪肉 (pig meat), 鸡肉 (chicken meat) 🥩

我不吃肉。
Wǒ bù chī ròu.
I don't eat meat.
