Today's word: 不好意思 (bù hǎoyìsi) = excuse me; I'm embarrassed 🐟🐟

不好意思 = excuse me, sorry, I'm a bit embarrassed 🙈 Lighter than 对不起. You'll hear it constantly!

不好意思，我来晚了。
Bù hǎoyìsi, wǒ lái wǎn le.
Sorry, I'm a bit late.
