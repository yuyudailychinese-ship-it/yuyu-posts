Today's word: 恭喜 (gōngxǐ) = congratulations 🐟🐟

恭喜 = congrats 🎊 At New Year people say 恭喜发财: "congratulations, get rich!"

恭喜你考试通过了！
Gōngxǐ nǐ kǎoshì tōngguò le!
Congratulations on passing your exam!

Say 恭喜 to someone who just achieved something 🎊
