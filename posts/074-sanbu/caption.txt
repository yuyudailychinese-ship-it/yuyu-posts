Today's word: 散步 (sànbù) = to take a walk 🐟🐟

Literally "loose steps" 🚶 A relaxed walk, often after dinner.

晚饭后我们去散步。
Wǎnfàn hòu wǒmen qù sànbù.
After dinner we go for a walk.
