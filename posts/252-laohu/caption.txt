Today's word: 老虎 (lǎohǔ) = tiger 🐟🐟

老虎 = tiger 🐯 The 老 doesn't mean old, it's just part of the name. Same in 老鼠 (mouse)!

老虎是很厉害的动物。
Lǎohǔ shì hěn lìhai de dòngwù.
Tigers are fierce animals.
