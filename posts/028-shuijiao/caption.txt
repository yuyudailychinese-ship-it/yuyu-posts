Today's word: 睡觉 (shuìjiào) = to sleep 🐟🐟

睡 starts with an eye 目 👁️ Close it, and you're 睡觉.

我十一点睡觉。
Wǒ shíyī diǎn shuìjiào.
I go to sleep at eleven.

What time do you 睡觉? 🌙
