Today's word: 警察 (jǐngchá) = police officer 🐟🐟

警 (warn) + 察 (inspect) = police 🚓 Pretty much the job description.

有问题可以问警察。
Yǒu wèntí kěyǐ wèn jǐngchá.
If you have a problem, you can ask the police.
