Today's word: 好看 (hǎokàn) = good-looking, nice to watch 🐟🐟

Literally "good to look at" 👀 Use it for a pretty outfit or a great movie.

这部电影很好看。
Zhè bù diànyǐng hěn hǎokàn.
This movie is really good.

A movie you think is really 好看? 🎬
