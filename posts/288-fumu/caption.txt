Today's word: 父母 (fùmǔ) = parents 🐟🐟

父 (father) + 母 (mother) = parents 👨‍👩 A whole family in two characters.

我父母住在北京。
Wǒ fùmǔ zhù zài Běijīng.
My parents live in Beijing.

What's one thing your 父母 always say? 👇
