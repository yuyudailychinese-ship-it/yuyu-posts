Today's word: 便利店 (biànlìdiàn) = convenience store 🐟🐟

Literally "convenience shop" 🏪 Exactly the same idea as in English.

楼下有一个便利店。
Lóuxià yǒu yí ge biànlìdiàn.
There's a convenience store downstairs.
