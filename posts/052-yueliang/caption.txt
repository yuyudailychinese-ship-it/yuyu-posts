Today's word: 月亮 (yuèliang) = moon 🐟🐟

Literally "bright moon" 🌕 And 月 also means month, because months follow the moon.

今天的月亮很圆。
Jīntiān de yuèliang hěn yuán.
The moon is very round tonight.
