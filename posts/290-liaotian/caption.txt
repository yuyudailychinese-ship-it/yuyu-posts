Today's word: 聊天 (liáotiān) = to chat 🐟🐟

Literally "chat about the sky" ☁️ Small talk about nothing and everything!

我们一边喝茶一边聊天。
Wǒmen yìbiān hē chá yìbiān liáotiān.
We chat while drinking tea.
