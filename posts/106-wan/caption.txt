Today's word: 玩 (wán) = to play, to have fun 🐟🐟

玩 = play, hang out, have fun 🎮 Adults say it all the time: 去哪儿玩?

周末你想去哪儿玩？
Zhōumò nǐ xiǎng qù nǎr wán?
Where do you want to go this weekend?

Where would you go to 玩 this weekend? 🎮
