Today's word: 妹妹 (mèimei) = younger sister 🐟🐟

妹妹 = little sister 🎀 姐姐 is older, 妹妹 is younger. Both have 女!

我妹妹很可爱。
Wǒ mèimei hěn kě'ài.
My little sister is very cute.
