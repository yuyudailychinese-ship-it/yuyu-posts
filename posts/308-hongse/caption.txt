Today's word: 红色 (hóngsè) = red 🐟🐟

红色 = red, the luckiest color in China 🧧 At New Year people give money in red envelopes, 红包.

红色是幸运的颜色。
Hóngsè shì xìngyùn de yánsè.
Red is a lucky color.

Ever received a 红包? 🧧
