Today's word: 工作 (gōngzuò) = work, job; to work 🐟🐟

工作 = work, job 💼 And 你做什么工作? is the classic small-talk question.

你做什么工作？
Nǐ zuò shénme gōngzuò?
What do you do for work?

Tell us: 我的工作是… 💼
