Today's word: 中午 (zhōngwǔ) = noon 🐟🐟

中午 = noon 🕛 上午 is before noon, 下午 after. Logical!

我们中午一起吃饭吧。
Wǒmen zhōngwǔ yìqǐ chīfàn ba.
Let's have lunch together at noon.
