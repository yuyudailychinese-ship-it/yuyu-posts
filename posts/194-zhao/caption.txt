Today's word: 找 (zhǎo) = to look for 🐟🐟

找 has the hand sign 扌 🔍 Searching usually involves your hands!

我在找我的钥匙。
Wǒ zài zhǎo wǒ de yàoshi.
I'm looking for my keys.

What do you always 找? Keys? Phone? 🔍
