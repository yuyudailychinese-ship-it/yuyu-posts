Today's word: 减肥 (jiǎnféi) = to lose weight, to diet 🐟🐟

Literally "reduce fat" 🥗 Very direct, very Chinese.

我在减肥，不吃蛋糕。
Wǒ zài jiǎnféi, bù chī dàngāo.
I'm on a diet, no cake for me.
