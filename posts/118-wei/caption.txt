Today's word: 喂 (wèi) = hello (on the phone); hey 🐟🐟

喂 = hello, but only when answering the phone 📞 Say it in person and it sounds like "hey you!"

喂，你好！
Wéi, nǐ hǎo!
Hello? Hi!

Answer the phone in Chinese: 喂! 📞
