Today's word: 家 (jiā) = home; family 🐟🐟

Memory trick: 家 is a roof 宀 with a pig 豕 under it 🐷 In ancient times, that's what a home looked like.

我想回家。
Wǒ xiǎng huí jiā.
I want to go home.
