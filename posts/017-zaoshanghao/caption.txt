Today's word: 早上好 (zǎoshang hǎo) = good morning 🐟🐟

Morning person? Just say 早 (zǎo) and you sound like a local ☀️

早上好！你吃早饭了吗？
Zǎoshang hǎo! Nǐ chī zǎofàn le ma?
Good morning! Have you had breakfast?
