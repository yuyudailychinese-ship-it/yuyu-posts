Today's word: 慢走 (màn zǒu) = take care (when someone leaves) 🐟🐟

Literally "walk slowly" 🚶 Said to guests leaving: "take care, don't rush."

再见，慢走！
Zàijiàn, màn zǒu!
Bye, take care!

Did you know 慢走 isn't about speed? 🚶
