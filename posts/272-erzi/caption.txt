Today's word: 儿子 (érzi) = son 🐟🐟

儿子 = son, 女儿 = daughter 👨‍👦 Add 女 (female) and you switch!

他们有一个儿子。
Tāmen yǒu yí ge érzi.
They have a son.

Learn both today: 儿子 and 女儿 ✍️
