Today's word: 好吃 (hǎochī) = delicious 🐟🐟

Literally "good to eat" 😋 Say it at any Chinese restaurant and watch the chef smile.

这个饺子很好吃！
Zhège jiǎozi hěn hǎochī!
These dumplings are delicious!
