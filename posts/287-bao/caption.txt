Today's word: 饱 (bǎo) = full (after eating) 🐟🐟

饿 = hungry, 饱 = full. Both have the food sign 饣 🍜 Learn them as a pair!

我吃饱了，谢谢！
Wǒ chībǎo le, xièxie!
I'm full, thank you!

Say 我吃饱了 if you just ate 😌
