Today's word: 冬天 (dōngtiān) = winter 🐟🐟

冬 has two dots at the bottom, like the ice sign ❄️ Winter is coming.

冬天这里很冷。
Dōngtiān zhèlǐ hěn lěng.
It's very cold here in winter.
