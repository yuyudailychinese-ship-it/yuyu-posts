Today's word: 十 (shí) = ten 🐟🐟

十 = ten 🔟 And Chinese numbers are so logical: 十一 is 11, 二十 is 20, 二十一 is 21.

我有十块钱。
Wǒ yǒu shí kuài qián.
I have ten yuan.
