Today's word: 好喝 (hǎohē) = tasty (drink) 🐟🐟

好吃 is for food, 好喝 is for drinks 🧋 "Good to drink"!

这杯奶茶真好喝！
Zhè bēi nǎichá zhēn hǎohē!
This milk tea is really tasty!
