Today's word: 厨师 (chúshī) = cook, chef 🐟🐟

Literally "kitchen master" 👨‍🍳 Same 师 as in 老师 (teacher).

他是一个很好的厨师。
Tā shì yí ge hěn hǎo de chúshī.
He is a very good chef.

Are you a 厨师 at home? 👨‍🍳
