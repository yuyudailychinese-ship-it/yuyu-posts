Today's word: 少 (shǎo) = few, little 🐟🐟

少 = few. And 多少 ("many few") = how many? 🤔 Chinese is fun.

今天人很少。
Jīntiān rén hěn shǎo.
There are very few people today.

多少钱? Save this for shopping 🛍️
