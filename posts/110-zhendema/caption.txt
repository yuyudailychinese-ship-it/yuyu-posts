Today's word: 真的吗 (zhēn de ma) = really? 🐟🐟

真的吗? = really?! 😲 Add 吗 to the end of a sentence and it becomes a yes/no question.

你会说五种语言？真的吗？
Nǐ huì shuō wǔ zhǒng yǔyán? Zhēn de ma?
You speak five languages? Really?

How many languages do you speak? 真的吗? 😲
