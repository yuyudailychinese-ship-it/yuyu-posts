Today's word: 男朋友 (nán péngyou) = boyfriend 🐟🐟

Literally "male friend" 💑 And 男 is a field 田 above strength 力: men worked the fields.

她有男朋友了。
Tā yǒu nánpéngyou le.
She has a boyfriend now.
