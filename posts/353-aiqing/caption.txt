Today's word: 爱情 (àiqíng) = love (romantic) 🐟🐟

爱情 is romantic love 💘 Fun fact: 520 (wǔ èr líng) sounds like 我爱你, so May 20 is a romantic day in China.

这是一个爱情故事。
Zhè shì yí ge àiqíng gùshi.
This is a love story.
