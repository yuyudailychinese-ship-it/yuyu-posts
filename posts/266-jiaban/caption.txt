Today's word: 加班 (jiābān) = to work overtime 🐟🐟

Literally "add a shift" 😩 Same 加 as in 加油 (add oil). You'll need both.

我今天要加班。
Wǒ jīntiān yào jiābān.
I have to work overtime today.

Send 加油 to everyone who has to 加班 😩
