Today's word: 伞 (sǎn) = umbrella 🐟🐟

伞 literally looks like an open umbrella ☂️ One of the best picture characters.

下雨了，你带伞了吗？
Xiàyǔ le, nǐ dài sǎn le ma?
It's raining, did you bring an umbrella?

Can you see the umbrella in 伞? ☂️
