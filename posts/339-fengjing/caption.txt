Today's word: 风景 (fēngjǐng) = scenery, landscape 🐟🐟

Literally "wind and view" 🏞️ Scenery you can feel and see.

这里的风景太美了！
Zhèlǐ de fēngjǐng tài měi le!
The scenery here is so beautiful!
