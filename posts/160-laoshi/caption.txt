Today's word: 老师 (lǎoshī) = teacher 🐟🐟

老师 doesn't mean your teacher is old 👩‍🏫 The 老 adds respect. Same in 老虎 (tiger)!

我们的老师很好。
Wǒmen de lǎoshī hěn hǎo.
Our teacher is great.

Thank a 老师 who helped you 🙏
