Today's word: 西瓜 (xīguā) = watermelon 🐟🐟

Literally "western melon" 🍉 It came to China from the west, so the name stuck.

夏天我们吃西瓜。
Xiàtiān wǒmen chī xīguā.
We eat watermelon in summer.

Summer without 西瓜? Impossible 🍉
