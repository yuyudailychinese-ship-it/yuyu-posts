Today's word: 爷爷 (yéye) = grandpa (father's side) 🐟🐟

爷爷 is your dad's dad 👴 Chinese grandparents have different names on each side of the family!

我爷爷八十岁了。
Wǒ yéye bāshí suì le.
My grandpa is eighty.
