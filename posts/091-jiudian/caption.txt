Today's word: 酒店 (jiǔdiàn) = hotel 🐟🐟

Literally "alcohol shop", but it means hotel 🏨 Don't be confused!

我们的酒店在海边。
Wǒmen de jiǔdiàn zài hǎibiān.
Our hotel is by the sea.

Would you have guessed 酒店 = hotel? 🏨
