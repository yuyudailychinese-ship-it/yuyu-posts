Today's word: 地址 (dìzhǐ) = address 🐟🐟

Chinese addresses start big and end small: country, city, street, number 📮 The opposite of English!

请给我你的地址。
Qǐng gěi wǒ nǐ de dìzhǐ.
Please give me your address.

Did you know addresses are reversed? 📮
