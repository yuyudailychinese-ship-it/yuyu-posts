Today's word: 小时 (xiǎoshí) = hour 🐟🐟

Literally "small time" ⏱️ An hour! (Old Chinese hours were twice as long.)

我每天学习一个小时。
Wǒ měitiān xuéxí yí ge xiǎoshí.
I study for an hour every day.

How many 小时 do you study a week? ⏱️
