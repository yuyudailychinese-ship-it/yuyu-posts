Today's word: 点菜 (diǎncài) = to order food 🐟🐟

Literally "point at dishes" 👉🍜 Which is honestly how ordering works.

我们可以点菜了吗？
Wǒmen kěyǐ diǎncài le ma?
Can we order now?
