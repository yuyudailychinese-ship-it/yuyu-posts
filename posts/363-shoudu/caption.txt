Today's word: 首都 (shǒudū) = capital (city) 🐟🐟

首都 = "head city" 🏛️ And 北京 means "northern capital", 南京 "southern capital".

北京是中国的首都。
Běijīng shì Zhōngguó de shǒudū.
Beijing is the capital of China.
