Today's word: 晴天 (qíngtiān) = sunny day 🐟🐟

晴 has 日 (sun) on the left ☀️ Sunny day, sunny character.

今天是晴天，我们出去玩吧！
Jīntiān shì qíngtiān, wǒmen chūqù wán ba!
It's sunny today, let's go out!
