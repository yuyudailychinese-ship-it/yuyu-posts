Today's word: 梦想 (mèngxiǎng) = dream (aspiration) 🐟🐟

梦 is a sleep dream, 梦想 is a life dream ✨ What's yours?

我的梦想是去中国旅游。
Wǒ de mèngxiǎng shì qù Zhōngguó lǚyóu.
