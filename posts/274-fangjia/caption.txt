Today's word: 放假 (fàngjià) = to have a holiday or vacation 🐟🐟

Literally "release a holiday" 🏖️ Best announcement ever.

我们下个星期放假。
Wǒmen xià ge xīngqī fàngjià.
We're off next week.

Next time you 放假, where are you going? 🏖️
