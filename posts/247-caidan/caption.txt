Today's word: 菜单 (càidān) = menu 🐟🐟

Literally "dish list" 📋 And at the end you ask for 买单, the bill.

服务员，请给我菜单。
Fúwùyuán, qǐng gěi wǒ càidān.
Waiter, the menu please.

Would you order from a Chinese 菜单? 📋
