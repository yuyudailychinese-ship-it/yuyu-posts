Today's word: 算了 (suànle) = forget it, never mind 🐟🐟

算了 = forget it, never mind 🙄 Literally "it's been counted": case closed.

算了，我们明天再说吧。
Suàn le, wǒmen míngtiān zài shuō ba.
Never mind, let's talk tomorrow.
