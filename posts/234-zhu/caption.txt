Today's word: 住 (zhù) = to live (reside) 🐟🐟

住 = a person 亻 + 主 (master) 🏠 A person being master of their place.

你住在哪儿？
Nǐ zhù zài nǎr?
Where do you live?

Answer in Chinese: 我住在… 👇
