Today's word: 以后 (yǐhòu) = after, later 🐟🐟

以后 = later, in the future ⏩ 以前 = before. Learn them as a pair!

以后我想住在中国。
Yǐhòu wǒ xiǎng zhù zài Zhōngguó.
In the future I want to live in China.

What do you want to do 以后? ⏩
