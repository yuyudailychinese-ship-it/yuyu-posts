Today's word: 粉色 (fěnsè) = pink 🐟🐟

粉 means powder and pink 🩷 And 粉丝 means both "fans" and glass noodles. Hi, 粉丝!

她的包是粉色的。
Tā de bāo shì fěnsè de.
Her bag is pink.
