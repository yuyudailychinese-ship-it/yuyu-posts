Today's word: 星期天 (xīngqītiān) = Sunday 🐟🐟

Sunday breaks the pattern: not "week seven", but 星期天 ("week day/sky") ☀️

星期天我在家休息。
Xīngqītiān wǒ zài jiā xiūxi.
On Sunday I rest at home.
