Today's word: 阿姨 (āyí) = aunt (also: friendly name for a woman) 🐟🐟

阿姨 = auntie 👩 Used for any older woman you're being polite to, like a shop owner.

阿姨，这个多少钱？
Āyí, zhège duōshao qián?
Excuse me ma'am, how much is this?
