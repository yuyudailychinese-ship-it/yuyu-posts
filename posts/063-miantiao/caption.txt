Today's word: 面条 (miàntiáo) = noodles 🐟🐟

Noodle strips 🍜 On birthdays people eat 长寿面, long-life noodles. Don't cut them!

他每天中午吃面条。
Tā měitiān zhōngwǔ chī miàntiáo.
He eats noodles every day at noon.
