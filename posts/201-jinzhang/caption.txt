Today's word: 紧张 (jǐnzhāng) = nervous; tense 🐟🐟

Literally "tight and stretched" 😬 Exactly how nerves feel.

考试的时候我很紧张。
Kǎoshì de shíhou wǒ hěn jǐnzhāng.
I get nervous during exams.

What makes you 紧张? 😬
