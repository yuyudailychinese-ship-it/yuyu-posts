Today's word: 风 (fēng) = wind 🐟🐟

风 = wind 🌬️ And 风水 (feng shui) is literally "wind and water".

今天风很大。
Jīntiān fēng hěn dà.
It's very windy today.

Did you know feng shui = 风水? 🌬️💧
