Today's word: 太阳 (tàiyáng) = sun 🐟🐟

太阳 = sun ☀️ That 阳 is the "yang" in yin and yang: bright and warm.

今天太阳很大。
Jīntiān tàiyáng hěn dà.
The sun is strong today.

Drop a ☀️ if the 太阳 is out where you are
