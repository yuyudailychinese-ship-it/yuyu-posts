Today's word: 冷 (lěng) = cold 🐟🐟

冷 has two dots 冫 on the left: the ice sign 🥶 Water 氵 has three dots, ice only two!

今天太冷了！
Jīntiān tài lěng le!
It's way too cold today!

Comment 冷 if it's cold where you are 🥶
