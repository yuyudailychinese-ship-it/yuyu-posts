Today's word: 饭馆 (fànguǎn) = restaurant 🐟🐟

Literally "meal hall" 🏮 The same 馆 appears in library (图书馆) and museum (博物馆).

这个饭馆很有名。
Zhège fànguǎn hěn yǒumíng.
This restaurant is very famous.
