Today's word: 羊 (yáng) = sheep, goat 🐟🐟

羊 = sheep or goat 🐑 Look closely: it's on top of 美 (beautiful). And 2027 is the Year of the Goat!

山上有很多羊。
Shān shang yǒu hěn duō yáng.
There are lots of sheep on the mountain.

Spot the 羊 in 美 and 鲜? 🐑
