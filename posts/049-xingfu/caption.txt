Today's word: 幸福 (xìngfú) = happiness; happy (life) 🐟🐟

幸福 is the deep kind of happy 🍀 The 福 is the one people hang upside down at New Year, because "upside down" (倒) sounds like "arrived" (到).

我们一家人很幸福。
Wǒmen yìjiārén hěn xìngfú.
Our family is very happy.

What makes you feel 幸福? 🍀
