Today's word: 上班 (shàngbān) = to go to work 🐟🐟

上班 = "go up to the shift", start work 💼 下班 = "come down", finish. Best word of the day!

我每天九点上班。
Wǒ měitiān jiǔ diǎn shàngbān.
I start work at nine every day.

What time do you 下班 today? ⏰
