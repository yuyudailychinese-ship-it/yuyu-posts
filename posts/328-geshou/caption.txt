Today's word: 歌手 (gēshǒu) = singer 🐟🐟

Literally "song hand" 🎤 手 (hand) can mean someone skilled at something.

她是一个有名的歌手。
Tā shì yí ge yǒumíng de gēshǒu.
She is a famous singer.
