Today's word: 西红柿 (xīhóngshì) = tomato 🐟🐟

Literally "western red persimmon" 🍅 And tomato-egg stir-fry is the dish every Chinese family knows.

西红柿炒鸡蛋很好吃。
Xīhóngshì chǎo jīdàn hěn hǎochī.
Stir-fried tomato and egg is delicious.
