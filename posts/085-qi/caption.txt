Today's word: 七 (qī) = seven 🐟🐟

七 = seven 💕 七夕 (the 7th night of the 7th month) is Chinese Valentine's Day.

一个星期有七天。
Yí ge xīngqī yǒu qī tiān.
A week has seven days.
