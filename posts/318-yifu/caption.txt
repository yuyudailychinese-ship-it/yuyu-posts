Today's word: 衣服 (yīfu) = clothes 🐟🐟

衣服 = clothes 👕 衤 (from 衣) shows up in many clothing words like 裤子 (pants).

我想买新衣服。
Wǒ xiǎng mǎi xīn yīfu.
I want to buy new clothes.
