Today's word: 饺子 (jiǎozi) = dumplings 🐟🐟

Dumplings! 🥟 Notice the food sign 饣 again, same as in 饿 (hungry).

过年的时候我们吃饺子。
Guònián de shíhou wǒmen chī jiǎozi.
We eat dumplings at Chinese New Year.

Honestly, how many 饺子 can you eat? 🥟
