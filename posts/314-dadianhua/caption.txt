Today's word: 打电话 (dǎ diànhuà) = to make a phone call 🐟🐟

Literally "hit the electric speech" 📞 Chinese uses 打 (hit) for lots of actions.

我给妈妈打电话。
Wǒ gěi māma dǎ diànhuà.
I'm calling my mom.

Who should you 打电话 today? 📞
