Today's word: 渴 (kě) = thirsty 🐟🐟

渴 has the water sign 氵 🥵 Because what do you need when you're thirsty?

我好渴，有水吗？
Wǒ hǎo kě, yǒu shuǐ ma?
I'm so thirsty, is there any water?
