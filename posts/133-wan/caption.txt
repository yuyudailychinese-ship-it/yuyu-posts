Today's word: 万 (wàn) = ten thousand 🐟🐟

Chinese counts in ten-thousands: 万 🤯 So a million is 一百万, "a hundred ten-thousands".

这个城市有一千万人。
Zhège chéngshì yǒu yìqiān wàn rén.
This city has ten million people.
