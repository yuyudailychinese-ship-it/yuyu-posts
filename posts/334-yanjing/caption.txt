Today's word: 眼镜 (yǎnjìng) = glasses (eyewear) 🐟🐟

Literally "eye lens" 👓 And you 戴 (wear) them, not 穿 (wear clothes).

我戴眼镜。
Wǒ dài yǎnjìng.
I wear glasses.
