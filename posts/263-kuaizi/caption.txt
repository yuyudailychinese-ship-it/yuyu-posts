Today's word: 筷子 (kuàizi) = chopsticks 🐟🐟

The ⺮ on top means bamboo 🥢 Chopsticks were made of it. Pro tip: never stick them upright in rice!

你会用筷子吗？
Nǐ huì yòng kuàizi ma?
