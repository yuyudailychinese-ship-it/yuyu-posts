Today's word: 姐姐 (jiějie) = older sister 🐟🐟

姐姐 = older sister 👧 Also used as a friendly "big sis" for young women.

我姐姐住在上海。
Wǒ jiějie zhù zài Shànghǎi.
My older sister lives in Shanghai.
