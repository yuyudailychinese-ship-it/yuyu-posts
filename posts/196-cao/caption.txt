Today's word: 草 (cǎo) = grass 🐟🐟

草 = grass 🌱 That 艹 on top is the grass sign, in 花, 菜, 茶 and more.

请不要在草上走。
Qǐng búyào zài cǎo shang zǒu.
Please don't walk on the grass.
