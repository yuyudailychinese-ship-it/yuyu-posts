Today's word: 冰淇淋 (bīngqílín) = ice cream 🐟🐟

冰 means ice, and 淇淋 (qílín) imitates the sound of "cream" 🍦 Ice + cream!

夏天我想吃冰淇淋。
Xiàtiān wǒ xiǎng chī bīngqílín.
In summer I want to eat ice cream.

One scoop or three? 🍦
