Today's word: 饭 (fàn) = meal; cooked rice 🐟🐟

In Chinese, "to eat" is often just 吃饭: eat rice 🍚 Rice = meal. Simple.

我们一起吃饭吧！
Wǒmen yìqǐ chīfàn ba!
Let's eat together!
