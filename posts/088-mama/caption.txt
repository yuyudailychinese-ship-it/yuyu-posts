Today's word: 妈妈 (māma) = mom 🐟🐟

妈 = woman 女 + horse 马 🐴 No, mom isn't a horse: 马 (mǎ) just gives the sound.

我很爱我妈妈。
Wǒ hěn ài wǒ māma.
I love my mom very much.
