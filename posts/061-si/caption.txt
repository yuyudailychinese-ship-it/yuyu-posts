Today's word: 四 (sì) = four 🐟🐟

四 is the unlucky number 😬 It sounds like 死 (death), so many buildings in China skip the 4th floor!

我家有四口人。
Wǒ jiā yǒu sì kǒu rén.
There are four people in my family.

Does your building have a 4th floor? 😬
