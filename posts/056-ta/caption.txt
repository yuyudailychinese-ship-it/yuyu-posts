Today's word: 他 (tā) = he, him 🐟🐟

他, 她, 它 all sound exactly the same: tā 🗣️ You only see the difference in writing.

他是我哥哥。
Tā shì wǒ gēge.
He is my older brother.

Which one is easiest to remember? 他 她 它
