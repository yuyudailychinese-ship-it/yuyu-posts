Today's word: 想念 (xiǎngniàn) = to miss (someone) 🐟🐟

想念 = to miss someone 💭 Casually, 我想你 means "I miss you".

我很想念我的家人。
Wǒ hěn xiǎngniàn wǒ de jiārén.
I really miss my family.
