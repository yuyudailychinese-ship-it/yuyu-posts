Today's word: 树 (shù) = tree 🐟🐟

树 has 木 (tree, wood) on the left 🌳 One 木 is a tree, 林 is woods, 森 is a forest!

这棵树很高。
Zhè kē shù hěn gāo.
This tree is very tall.

Comment 木 林 森 to remember the trio 🌳
