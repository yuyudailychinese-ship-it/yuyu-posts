Today's word: 手表 (shǒubiǎo) = wristwatch 🐟🐟

手表 = "hand meter" ⌚ Tip: never gift a clock (钟) in China. 送钟 sounds like attending a funeral (送终)!

我的手表停了。
Wǒ de shǒubiǎo tíng le.
My watch has stopped.
