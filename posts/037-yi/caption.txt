Today's word: 一 (yī) = one 🐟🐟

一 is one line 1️⃣ Its tone changes in phrases: yí ge, yì bēi. Don't worry, you'll pick it up!

我有一个哥哥。
Wǒ yǒu yí ge gēge.
I have one older brother.
