Today's word: 白色 (báisè) = white 🐟🐟

白色 = white 🤍 Traditionally it's the color of funerals in China, so avoid white gift wrapping!

我想买白色的鞋。
Wǒ xiǎng mǎi báisè de xié.
I want to buy white shoes.
