Today's word: 女朋友 (nǚ péngyou) = girlfriend 🐟🐟

Literally "female friend" 💕 Careful: 女朋友 always means girlfriend, not just a friend who's a girl!

他的女朋友很漂亮。
Tā de nǚpéngyou hěn piàoliang.
His girlfriend is very pretty.
