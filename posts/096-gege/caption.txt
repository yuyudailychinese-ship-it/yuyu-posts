Today's word: 哥哥 (gēge) = older brother 🐟🐟

English says "brother", Chinese asks: older or younger? 哥哥 is the older one 👦

我有一个哥哥。
Wǒ yǒu yí ge gēge.
I have an older brother.

Are you the oldest, middle or youngest? 👇
