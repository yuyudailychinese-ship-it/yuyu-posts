Today's word: 零 (líng) = zero 🐟🐟

零 = zero 0️⃣ Bonus: in phone numbers, 1 is said yāo so it doesn't sound like 七 (qī).

我的电话号码是零一二三…
Wǒ de diànhuà hàomǎ shì líng yāo èr sān…
My phone number is 0123…

Did you know the yāo trick? 0️⃣
