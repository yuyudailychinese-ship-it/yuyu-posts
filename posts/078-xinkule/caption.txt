Today's word: 辛苦了 (xīnkǔ le) = thanks for your hard work 🐟🐟

辛苦了 = "you worked hard" 🙏 A warm thank-you with no perfect English version.

今天辛苦了！
Jīntiān xīnkǔ le!
Thanks for all your hard work today!

Say 辛苦了 to someone who worked hard today 🙏
