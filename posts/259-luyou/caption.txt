Today's word: 旅游 (lǚyóu) = to travel; tourism 🐟🐟

旅游 = travel ✈️ 游 means to wander (or swim), so traveling is wandering the world.

我喜欢旅游。
Wǒ xǐhuan lǚyóu.
I love traveling.
