Today's word: 礼物 (lǐwù) = gift, present 🐟🐟

礼物 = gift 🎁 In China, people often politely refuse a gift once or twice before accepting!

这是给你的礼物。
Zhè shì gěi nǐ de lǐwù.
This is a gift for you.
