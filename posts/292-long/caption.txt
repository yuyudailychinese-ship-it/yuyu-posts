Today's word: 龙 (lóng) = dragon 🐟🐟

龙 = dragon 🐉 Unlike Western dragons, Chinese dragons are lucky and bring rain!

龙是中国的象征。
Lóng shì Zhōngguó de xiàngzhēng.
The dragon is a symbol of China.
