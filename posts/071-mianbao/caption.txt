Today's word: 面包 (miànbāo) = bread 🐟🐟

Literally "flour bun" 🍞 Chinese words are often just honest descriptions.

早上我吃了一个面包。
Zǎoshang wǒ chīle yí ge miànbāo.
I ate a bread roll this morning.
