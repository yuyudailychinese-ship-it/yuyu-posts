Today's word: 超市 (chāoshì) = supermarket 🐟🐟

超 = super, 市 = market. 超市 = supermarket 🛒 A direct translation!

我去超市买水果。
Wǒ qù chāoshì mǎi shuǐguǒ.
I'm going to the supermarket to buy fruit.
