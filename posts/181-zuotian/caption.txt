Today's word: 昨天 (zuótiān) = yesterday 🐟🐟

昨天 = yesterday ⏪ Chinese verbs don't change for the past. Just say 昨天!

昨天我很忙。
Zuótiān wǒ hěn máng.
I was very busy yesterday.
