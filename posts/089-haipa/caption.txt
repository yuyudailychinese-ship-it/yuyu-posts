Today's word: 害怕 (hàipà) = to be afraid 🐟🐟

怕 has the heart sign 忄 on the left 😱 Fear is felt in the heart!

我害怕蛇。
Wǒ hàipà shé.
I'm afraid of snakes.

What are you 害怕 of? 🕷️🐍🤡
