Today's word: 鱼 (yú) = fish 🐟🐟

Hey, that's us! 🐟 鱼鱼 = two little fish. At New Year people wish 年年有余 (niánnián yǒu yú): plenty every year.

我喜欢吃鱼。
Wǒ xǐhuan chī yú.
I like eating fish.
