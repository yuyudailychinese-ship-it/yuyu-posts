Today's word: 没问题 (méi wèntí) = no problem 🐟🐟

Literally "no problem" 👍 Same as English! And 问题 means both question and problem.

明天见？没问题！
Míngtiān jiàn? Méi wèntí!
See you tomorrow? No problem!
