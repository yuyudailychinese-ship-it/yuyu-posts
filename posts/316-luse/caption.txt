Today's word: 绿色 (lǜsè) = green 🐟🐟

绿色 = green 💚 Fun warning: never give a man a green hat. 戴绿帽子 means his partner cheated!

我喜欢绿色。
Wǒ xǐhuan lǜsè.
I like green.
