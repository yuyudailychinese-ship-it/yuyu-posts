Today's word: 认真 (rènzhēn) = serious, conscientious 🐟🐟

认真 = serious, conscientious ✍️ Calling someone 认真 is a real compliment in China.

他学习很认真。
Tā xuéxí hěn rènzhēn.
He studies very seriously.

Comment 认真 if you study every day ✍️
