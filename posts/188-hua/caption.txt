Today's word: 花 (huā) = flower; to spend 🐟🐟

花 = flower 🌸 But also "to spend": 花钱 means spending money. Flowers do cost money!

春天花都开了。
Chūntiān huā dōu kāi le.
In spring the flowers all bloom.
