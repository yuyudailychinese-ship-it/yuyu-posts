Today's word: 出租车 (chūzūchē) = taxi 🐟🐟

Literally "rent-out car" 🚕 In Chinese you "sit" (坐) a taxi.

我们坐出租车去吧。
Wǒmen zuò chūzūchē qù ba.
Let's take a taxi.
