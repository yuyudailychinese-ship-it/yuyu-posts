Today's word: 老板 (lǎobǎn) = boss, shop owner 🐟🐟

老板 = boss 💼 In shops and street stalls, people call the owner 老板 too.

老板今天不在。
Lǎobǎn jīntiān bú zài.
The boss isn't in today.
