Today's word: 便宜 (piányi) = cheap 🐟🐟

便宜 = cheap 💸 Tricky: 便 is pián here, but biàn in 方便 (convenient).

这里的东西很便宜。
Zhèlǐ de dōngxi hěn piányi.
Things here are cheap.
