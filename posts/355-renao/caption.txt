Today's word: 热闹 (rènao) = lively, bustling 🐟🐟

Literally "hot and noisy" 🏮 The perfect word for a busy night market.

晚上的夜市很热闹。
Wǎnshang de yèshì hěn rènao.
The night market is very lively in the evening.
