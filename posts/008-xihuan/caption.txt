Today's word: 喜欢 (xǐhuan) = to like 🐟🐟

喜 (joy) + 欢 (happy). Liking something is double happiness 😊

我喜欢喝茶。
Wǒ xǐhuan hē chá.
I like drinking tea.

Tell us one thing you 喜欢 👇
