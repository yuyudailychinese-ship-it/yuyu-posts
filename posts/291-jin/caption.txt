Today's word: 近 (jìn) = near, close 🐟🐟

近 = near 📍 Walking sign 辶 + 斤: a short walk away.

超市很近，走路五分钟。
Chāoshì hěn jìn, zǒulù wǔ fēnzhōng.
The supermarket is close, a 5-minute walk.
