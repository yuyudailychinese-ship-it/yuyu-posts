Today's word: 同事 (tóngshì) = colleague 🐟🐟

Literally "same work" 💼 同学 at school, 同事 at work.

我的同事都很热情。
Wǒ de tóngshì dōu hěn rèqíng.
My colleagues are all very friendly.
