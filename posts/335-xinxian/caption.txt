Today's word: 新鲜 (xīnxiān) = fresh 🐟🐟

Look at 鲜: it's 鱼 (fish) + 羊 (sheep) 🐟🐑 Fresh fish and lamb, the tastiest things. Our kind of character!

这些鱼很新鲜。
Zhèxiē yú hěn xīnxiān.
These fish are very fresh.
