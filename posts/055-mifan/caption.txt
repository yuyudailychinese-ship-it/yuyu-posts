Today's word: 米饭 (mǐfàn) = cooked rice 🐟🐟

米 = rice grains, 米饭 = rice ready to eat 🍚 Bonus: 米 also means meter!

我要一碗米饭。
Wǒ yào yì wǎn mǐfàn.
I'd like a bowl of rice.
