Today's word: 醒 (xǐng) = to wake up 🐟🐟

醒 = wake up 😴 It has the jar sign 酉 like 酒 (alcohol), because it also means sobering up!

你醒了吗？
Nǐ xǐng le ma?
Are you awake?

Comment 醒 if you just woke up ☀️
