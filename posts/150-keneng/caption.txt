Today's word: 可能 (kěnéng) = maybe; possible 🐟🐟

可能 = maybe 🤔 Add 不 for 不可能: impossible! (Nothing is 不可能.)

明天可能会下雨。
Míngtiān kěnéng huì xiàyǔ.
It might rain tomorrow.

Comment 一切皆有可能: anything is possible 🤔
