Today's word: 医生 (yīshēng) = doctor 🐟🐟

In Chinese you "look at" a doctor: 看医生 🩺 And 医院 is a hospital, "heal building".

我感冒了，要去看医生。
Wǒ gǎnmào le, yào qù kàn yīshēng.
I have a cold, I need to see a doctor.

Ever been to a 医生 abroad? 🩺
